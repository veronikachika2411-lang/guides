Reel Studio / concept guide

One line.
A whole world.

An education story that moves from a word pair to a connected planet.

Duolingo is a language-learning app that also offers other subjects. This is an unofficial concept by Veronika. Duolingo did not commission it.

Materials first: supplied brand visuals, a vector owl logo, generated fire and owl artwork, and Veronika's approved ending sound or your own licensed replacement. No voice. No licensed music track in the accepted version: its music and effects are synthesised.

01 / brief

Let the line lead

One lime line connects words, practice days, subjects and people. It is also the transition rule: the next scene opens behind a line that sweeps across the frame.

0-5.5 sPlanet horizon and greetings, then the owl rises like a sun.
5.5-12.7 sPhone word pairing, learning path and the five-section comparison.
12.7-21.1 sProfessor joke, streak year, Lily call.
21.1-28.1 sApp icon wall, a continuous Languages / Math / Music / Chess panorama, dotted globe.
28.1-30.6 sReturn to horizon, “Hasta mañana,” and the wink with the ending sound.
Visual rules: recognisable vector owl, lime #58CC02 as the recurring line, different scene colours, dots for the planet and a shader atmosphere. The source uses 919 frames at 30 fps. Main transitions fall at frames 0, 90, 169, 249, 315, 381, 447, 540, 633, 699, 752, 844 and 919; do not infer the length of each small scene from the five broad groups above. Use 3D selectively for path buttons, figures, the dotted year, app tiles and notes.

If using the source's claims, qualify them: the five-semester comparison is from Duolingo's own 2021 study and concerns reading and listening only; 3.6x is a company-reported association with course completion after a seven-day streak, not a learning-speed or causal claim; the 140 million figure is rounded from the company's Q2 2026 monthly-user report. Ask me for the visual assets and approved ending sound. Make original synthesised music and effects. Do not assume a music service or licensed track. Render a draft and ask me to inspect motion and sound myself. Label it an unofficial concept by Veronika, not commissioned by Duolingo.

03 / breakdown and checks

Check every claim

Why this reel works

The line gives the eye one continuous object to follow while the subject changes. The opening word pair makes the final “Hasta mañana” understandable. The sequence grows from one action on a phone to a larger picture of connected learners.

What is editorial

The jokes, quoted dialogue and “Learning that works” are written for the reel. They are not official brand copy. The planet outlines are simplified artwork. Fire and owl-with-knife images are generated assets.

What to verify in the draft

  • Keep the lime line visible through each scene transition. Check it does not jump or disappear.
  • Keep the five-semester statement restricted to reading and listening, and the 3.6x statement restricted to course completion association.
  • Show the paid Max context for Lily's video-call feature.
  • Check rights to the logo, generated images, app icons and ending sound before wider sharing.
  • Listen to the final wink sound and inspect motion locally.
Source limit: the ready prompt is reconstructed from the scenario and source code. No single original production prompt was found, and this prompt has not been tested as a one-shot recreation.
