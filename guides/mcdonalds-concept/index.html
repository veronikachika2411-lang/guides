Reel Studio / concept guide

From arches
to appetite.

Materials first: approved food images or newly generated alternatives for the burger layers, cheese, bun, fries, nuggets, meal and McFlurry; generated food video for the niche, cheese pull and falling fries; a checked location dataset if showing a restaurant count; a permitted voice and music or original replacements. Check rights to every brand mark and asset.

A food story travels from the arches to a meal, then ends on a broken ice-cream machine joke.

McDonald's is a restaurant brand known for burgers, fries and other fast food. This is an unofficial concept by Veronika. McDonald's did not commission it.

Remotion / generated food imagery / 1080 x 1920 / about 27 seconds
01 / brief

Make one chain of ideas

The arches become a burger frame. Cheese becomes a wall. A bun becomes a switch, then a clock. Clock rays become fries. Location pins gather into the arches, whose legs become a road.

0-2.4 sArches and a restaurant counter in America.
2.4-6.7 sBurger layers land to the words patties, sauce, lettuce, cheese, pickles, onions, bun. Cheese fills the screen.
6.7-12.9 sFood niche, cheese pull, bun switch and a clock that says anytime.
12.9-17.8 sClock becomes a fries burst, falling fries, then four nugget shapes.
17.8-22.1 sUS location pins become the arches, a road and a meal.
22.1-27.4 sMcFlurry request, broken machine joke, logo and slogan.
Visual system: red #DA291C, yellow #FFC72C, cheese #e7bc4e, deep red vignette. Barlow Condensed heavy italic carries the ad words. Its source beat function uses 14.4 frames per beat at 30 fps, with offsets after the opening and near the end. The delivered reel is about 27.4 seconds.

Claude Code / reconstructed prompt
Read FIRST-REEL.md and EXAMPLES.md. Help me make an unofficial McDonald's concept reel by Veronika from this guide.

Create a full-frame vertical Remotion concept ad. Start with arches and a restaurant counter only if we can document the count and date. Assemble a burger layer by layer to the matching words. Let cheese cover the frame and reveal a burger niche, then a close cheese pull. Turn a bun into a switch and clock. Let its rays become fries, then a falling stream. Show nugget shapes. Bring location pins together into arches, stretch the legs into a road and reveal a meal. End with a McFlurry request, a broken-machine joke, then the arches.

Use the source's red and yellow palette and heavy condensed italic type. Keep ad text sharp. Ask me for approved food images, video, location data, voice and music rights, or help create original replacements. Treat the machine gag as fiction. Do not include any production-time claim or restaurant count unless I supply dated evidence; omit both by default. Do not copy an unverified restaurant count or imply the generated food is product photography. Render a draft, show stills and ask me to inspect motion and sound. Label the work an unofficial concept by Veronika, not commissioned by McDonald's.

Useful revision notes

“Carry the same bun from the switch into the clock.”

“Let the clock rays become the fries, without a disconnected cut.”

“Make the pins form the arches before the road appears.”

03 / checks

Protect the claims

  • Do not claim how fast the reel was made unless you can document it; the original reel and cover carried a time claim that the production note says was too short.
  • The source shows a restaurant count. Its data file is a project snapshot, not a current audited count. Verify the location, date and method, or omit the number.
  • Treat the broken machine and “You have arrived” as story copy, not a statement about service availability.
  • Check permissions for the generated food, original music, voice, logo and brand words before use.
  • Inspect the finished motion and listen to the final sound yourself.
