01 / references first

Collect motion before you write

My first four versions were made "from my head" and got rejected: too childish, boring, unclear, too simple. What fixed it was a board of real motion references.

  • Collect 10 to 15 references: official brand motion, app ads with a 3D camera, transitions you love. Screen-record them.
  • Write down the trick, not the vibe: "the logo assembles from dots", "white light ribbons fold into the arcs", "a carousel of player cards", "a button that explodes into rings".
  • Give every reference one scene and say why it belongs there. A table "reference → scene → why" is the most useful page of the whole project.
Keep what already worked. When a version is rejected, change the one thing that failed. Bright brand color pairs, duotone album art and the giant logo arcs survived every round.
02 / structure

Hook, app, features, logo

1Hook: the logo builds itself. Dots jump on the beat, stretch into three light ribbons, the ribbons fold into the arcs, the circle fills. The camera flies through the logo into the next scene.
2One line of meaning: "Every moment has a sound."
3Moments, each with a different picture and faster cuts: Late night, Morning run, Road trip.
4The drop: a ring of real covers spins and collapses into one app. "100,000,000 songs. One app."
5Features, one line each, shown with real interface: daylist, Blend, Jam.
6The logo builds a second time, in a new way. Slogan.

Official tone, no jokes. Use real numbers only and check them. Do not quote song lyrics: show a title and an artist instead.

03 / premium picture

What makes it look expensive

  • A real 3D camera in every scene. Orbit, tilt, fly through, a small kick on every hit. In Remotion this is plain CSS 3D: one camera container, scenes as flat planes at different depths.
  • One line does every transition. A glowing line crosses the frame and the next scene opens behind it.
  • Real material in brand colors. Real album covers in duotone, bright color pairs per scene, the logo arcs as giant graphics.
  • Every scene has its own layout. Never two scenes in a row with the same composition.
  • A strong font. A bold geometric face like Figtree Black, not a default system font.
  • Text stays sharp. Headlines rise from a mask. No blur, no shake.

05 / checks

Check before you show anyone

  • Render a contact sheet of frames: nothing overlaps, no text touches the edge, no two scenes in a row look the same.
  • Judge the level on a short video, not on stills. Motion is the product here.
  • Check every number and the rights to fonts, covers, sounds and music.
  • Label it an unofficial concept. Do not imply the brand paid for it.
  • Listen to the sound and watch the motion yourself. A still frame cannot show either.
