01 / brief

Every cut goes through the product

A set of pictures isn’t a reel. Here the dial of one shot lands exactly on the dial of the next, and only the angle changes.

0-2 sMacro of the crown: “Pull the crown.” Then “TIME stopped.”, a lime seconds hand and a small stopped timer.
2-4 sWhite dial on a tan strap: “STILL chronograph.”
4-8 s“YOUR dial.”: a wipe turns white to black. “YOUR strap.”: black alligator, chocolate suede, navy NATO, oyster steel.
8-13 sClose-up and three-quarter view. A light strip passes over. “12.4 MM PROFILE.”
13-16 sA cut through a water drop, splashes: “TIME goes on.”
16-20 sMacro of the dial, then the watch fades down: “Make it yours.” and the name.
Look: black backgrounds, steel, one lime accent on the seconds hand. Anton with Bodoni Moda italic, Geist Mono for small type. Slow camera moves, no shake.

03 / checks

What to look at

  • One watch: generated photos often change the hands or the case. Ask Claude to reject those that don’t match.
  • Dial cuts: if the circles don’t line up, it reads as a slideshow.
  • Colors: acid colors look wrong on a watch. Use muted ones, with one accent.
  • Bright studio: looks like a toy. Keep it dark, with light on the edges.
  • Specs: 12.4 mm is made up for this brand. For a real product, use its real numbers.
  • Sound and motion: Claude can’t hear or watch video. Check them yourself.
How the original was made: it used separate textures and masks for each strap and dial, not 6 to 8 full photos. The photo route in this guide is an alternative, so your result will differ. The original differs from what this guide will give you. Your reel will differ from the original.
