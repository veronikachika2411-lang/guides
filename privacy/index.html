Privacy

This is a personal site run by Veronika, the author of the Instagram account @veroniixx.ai. The short version: the site does not track you.

What this site collects

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, ad pixels or tracking scripts.
  • One form: your email, if you ask for a guide. Nothing else, no name, no account.
  • Fonts, videos and PDF files are served from this site, not from third parties.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic technical data when your browser loads a page, and may keep it in server logs for security. I do not have access to those logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to other services

Buttons that lead to Instagram, Telegram or other sites open those services, and their own privacy policies apply from there. If you message me, I use what you send only to reply to you.

Changes

Your email

When you ask for a guide, your email and the name of the guide are saved to a private Google Sheet that only I can open, and Google sends you the guide from my Gmail. I use your email for two things: to send that guide, and one short email when a new guide is out. I never sell or share it. To unsubscribe or have your email deleted, reply "stop" to any email from me, and I remove it. Google processes this data under the Google privacy policy.

If the site starts collecting anything else, this page will say so before it happens.
